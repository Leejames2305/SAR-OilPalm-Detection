## Focus Lens: Data Expansion (External GEE & New Covariates)

Prioritize expanding the feature space with **genuinely new** leak-free tree-level covariates for all `7,220` `(estate, Long, Lat)` rows into a new `/app/datasets/<name>/features.parquet` (total `/app/datasets/` under `128 MB` for automatic carry-over):

1. **Do not duplicate existing datasets:** First check `/app/datasets/`. If local ALOS-2 window statistics (e.g. `alos_winstats`) already exist, treat local ALOS-2 extraction as complete and move **immediately** to external Google Earth Engine extraction rather than re-extracting or re-debugging existing local SAR tables.
2. **Primary deliverable — Google Earth Engine extraction (`agentrsi gcp-init`):** Run `agentrsi gcp-init` early, consult `/app/prior/Plan-GEECatalogSurvey.md`, and batch-sample (`reduceRegions` per estate, chunked to stay fast and under GEE memory limits) all three estates uniformly:
   - **Sentinel-2 red-edge & SWIR stress stack (`COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED`):** Cloud/SCL-masked surface reflectance (`B2–B8, B8A, B11, B12`; note `S2_SR_HARMONIZED` aligns baseline 04.00+ DN offsets to pre-2022 scale), key canopy-stress indices (`NDRE1/2, NDMI, NBR, CI_rededge, MSI, EVI`), multi-window temporal change/dispersion (e.g., recent 6-month vs prior 6-month delta, IQR/std), and **local-contrast** features (tree pixel minus local 3×3 or 5×5 neighborhood median, which cancel estate-wide age/lighting trends).
   - **Topography & drainage:** `COPERNICUS/DEM/GLO30` or `USGS/SRTMGL1_003` (and `MERIT/Hydro/v1_0_1` if available) for elevation, slope, relative local elevation, and drainage/HAND proxies linked to waterlogging and *Ganoderma* spread.
   - **Optional secondary sensor (if time permits):** `COPERNICUS/S1_GRD` 10 m C-band VV/VH median, ratio, and temporal std/delta across all three estates.
3. **Evaluation & modeling:** Save `features.parquet`, `extract.py`, and `MANIFEST.md` (with exact GEE collection IDs, date windows, and masking logic) under `/app/datasets/<name>/` even if a model variant is rejected. For every new feature family, check cross-estate Cohen's $d$ sign consistency and paired 80 m spatial-block CV lift (`agentrsi cv`) against both the incumbent and coordinates-only baseline; test compact regularized linear, residual-on-incumbent, and/or shallow tree models (`requirements.txt` pinned if non-base) targeting consistent improvement across at least 2 of 3 estates.
