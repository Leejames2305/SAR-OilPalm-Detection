# Label-free multi-date SAR patch representations

Source: provided ROI ALOS-2 June 10 and October 14 2026 GeoTIFFs, exact coordinates for all 7,220 trees. `extract.py` reprojects WGS84 into every raster CRS and bilinearly samples a common 15x15 grid at 6.43m centred on each trunk. Four log10-power, H/A/alpha (alpha divided by 90), and four Yamaguchi dB bands per date: 22 channels. Power and HAlpha use positive finite masks, Yamaguchi uses finite and co-registered power support (negative dB retained). Invalid pixels are filled with channel ROI median before interpolation. Values are robustly globally scaled and clipped [-6,6]. No labels are read for feature extraction or representation fitting.

`train_gpu.py` trains two compact CNNs on all images without labels: VICReg same-patch rotated/flipped/noisy views, and masked denoising reconstruction. 121 and 101 epochs, seed 20261125, T4 GPU. Encoders pool global and central representations and average four rotations. 128 dimensions per encoder, compressed label-free with globally standardised PCA to 32 each. `assemble.py` writes 65 numerical features plus exact keys. Zero NaNs/duplicate keys. Label-free transductive preprocessing uses target imagery and no target labels. It does not prove transferable disease signal; downstream block validation is mandatory.

Reproduce: run extract.py, upload resulting /run/agentrsi/colab-io/in/sar_patches.npz and execute train_gpu.py on a GPU; copy embeddings and encoder weights to expected job output or adapt assemble.py source. CPU evaluation uses only features.parquet, without torch/rasterio/network. Pretrained weights, transforms and raw embeddings included for provenance. Overlapping crops share unlabeled imagery; labels are only used in downstream spatial folds.
